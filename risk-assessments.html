Professional Risk Assessments for Your Business

Ambergreen Safety provides workplace Health & Safety risk assessments in Worthing, West Sussex and surrounding Sussex areas. We identify significant hazards, consider who could be harmed, assess existing controls and recommend sensible additional controls where required.

The aim is a suitable and sufficient, usable risk assessment that reflects your actual work rather than generic paperwork.

Types of Risk Assessment

  • General workplace and office risk assessments
  • Specific activity and task risk assessments
  • COSHH assessments
  • Manual handling assessments
  • Work at height assessments
  • Work equipment and machinery assessments
  • Lone working assessments
  • Construction and site activity risk assessments
  • Vehicle and workplace transport assessments
  • Fire safety and emergency-related risk considerations

Who We Help

We support small and medium-sized businesses, contractors and construction companies looking for a Health & Safety consultant in Worthing or practical risk assessment support across West Sussex, including surrounding areas such as Shoreham-by-Sea, Lancing, Littlehampton, Angmering, Rustington and nearby communities.

Risk Assessment Review

Risk assessments should be reviewed when there is a significant change, following an incident or when experience indicates that existing controls may no longer be adequate. We can review existing assessments and identify where improvements are needed.
