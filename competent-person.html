Health & Safety Competent Person Service

Professional Health & Safety support without employing a full-time adviser.

Professional support when you need it

Our Competent Person Service gives your business access to professional Health & Safety advice when you need it, with support tailored to your size and activities.

What the service can provide

  • Ongoing Health & Safety advice by telephone and email
  • Review of your Health & Safety policy
  • Review of risk assessments and documentation
  • Advice on legislation and requirements
  • Assistance with procedures
  • Advice following accidents, incidents or near misses
  • Health & Safety inspections and reviews
  • Assistance with CHAS and Constructionline
  • Regular status reports and practical consultancy

Why use a Competent Person Service?

You have access to professional Health & Safety advice without the cost of employing a full-time Health & Safety manager.

Practical, accessible and proportionate

We believe businesses should receive the Health & Safety support they actually need. There is no need to create unnecessary paperwork or complicated systems.

How much does the service cost?
The cost depends on the size of your business, the nature of your activities and the support you require. Contact Ambergreen Safety for a straightforward quotation.
