Health & Safety Competent Person Service

Ongoing professional Health & Safety support without employing a full-time Health & Safety adviser.

What is a Health & Safety Competent Person?

Businesses need access to competent Health & Safety assistance appropriate to their activities. Ambergreen Safety can provide an external Competent Person service for businesses that need professional advice but do not require a full-time Health & Safety manager.

What our Competent Person service includes

  • Ongoing Health & Safety advice
  • Health & Safety Policy reviews
  • Risk assessment and RAMS reviews
  • Health & Safety procedures and documentation
  • Advice on workplace Health & Safety requirements
  • Accident, incident and near-miss advice
  • Workplace inspections and Health & Safety audits
  • Support with SSIP accreditation and pre-qualification
  • Construction Health & Safety and CDM advice
  • Practical recommendations and follow-up

Who is the service for?

The service is particularly suitable for small and medium-sized businesses and contractors who need reliable Health & Safety support without the cost and commitment of employing a full-time adviser.

Worthing & Sussex Health & Safety support

Ambergreen Safety provides Competent Person support in Worthing, West Sussex, Hove, Littlehampton and surrounding Sussex areas.

Proportionate and practical

Our approach is to provide the level of Health & Safety support appropriate to your business, activities and risks. The objective is effective Health & Safety management, not unnecessary paperwork.
