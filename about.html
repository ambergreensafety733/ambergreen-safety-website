About Ambergreen Safety

Practical Health & Safety advice, guidance and support for businesses and contractors.

Practical Health & Safety advice

Ambergreen Safety is a Health & Safety consultancy serving businesses and contractors in Worthing, West Sussex and surrounding Sussex areas. We provide practical advice, documentation, risk assessments, training and ongoing Health & Safety support.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the work, identify significant risks, recommend sensible controls and help the business maintain arrangements that are effective and proportionate.

Our approach to Health & Safety

Straightforward advice

We explain Health & Safety requirements in clear, practical language rather than unnecessary jargon.

Practical solutions

We focus on controls and arrangements that can work in the real workplace.

Proportionate systems

Health & Safety arrangements should reflect the size, activities and risks of the business.

Personal service

We work with clients to understand their business and provide support relevant to their actual needs.

Sectors we support

  • Construction and contractors
  • Manufacturing
  • Transport and logistics
  • Waste management and recycling
  • Healthcare
  • Office and commercial workplaces
  • Small and medium-sized businesses

Based in Worthing, serving Sussex

Ambergreen Safety provides Health & Safety consultancy and support in Worthing and throughout surrounding West Sussex and parts of East Sussex, including Hove and Littlehampton.
