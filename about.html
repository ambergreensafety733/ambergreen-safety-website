About Ambergreen Safety

Taking the mystery out of Health & Safety.

Practical Health & Safety advice

Ambergreen Safety is a Sussex-based Health & Safety consultancy providing practical advice, guidance and training to businesses and contractors.

We don't believe Health & Safety should create unnecessary barriers to running a successful business. Good Health & Safety is about identifying significant risks, putting sensible controls in place and helping people work safely.

Our approach

Straightforward advice

We explain requirements in plain English.

Practical solutions

We concentrate on controls that work in the real world.

Proportionate systems

Your arrangements should reflect the size and activities of your business.

Personal service

We work with you to understand your business and what you need to achieve.
