SSIP Accreditation & Contractor Pre-Qualification Support

Practical help for businesses and contractors applying for or maintaining Health & Safety accreditation.

What is SSIP accreditation?

SSIP means Safety Schemes in Procurement. SSIP schemes provide a recognised approach to assessing a contractor's Health & Safety arrangements for procurement and pre-qualification purposes. The exact requirements depend on the scheme and the work being undertaken.

Ambergreen Safety helps businesses understand what information is required and prepare appropriate evidence without creating unnecessary paperwork.

CHAS, SafeContractor & Constructionline

CHAS

Support with reviewing Health & Safety policies, risk assessments, procedures, training and other evidence needed for application or renewal.

SafeContractor

Practical assistance with the Health & Safety information and evidence requested for registration, accreditation or renewal.
