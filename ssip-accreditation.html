SSIP & Contractor Health & Safety Accreditation Support

Practical help with Health & Safety accreditations and pre-qualification, including CHAS, SafeContractor and Constructionline.

What is SSIP accreditation?

SSIP stands for Safety Schemes in Procurement. SSIP is intended to help reduce unnecessary duplication in Health & Safety pre-qualification by providing a recognised assessment of a contractor's Health & Safety arrangements.

In practice, clients and principal contractors may ask you to hold, or apply for, an appropriate Health & Safety accreditation before you are considered for work. The information required can include your policy, risk assessments, training and competence records, accident arrangements, procedures and other supporting evidence.
