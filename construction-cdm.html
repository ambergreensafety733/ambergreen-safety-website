CDM Health & Safety Support

The Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 place duties on those involved in construction projects. We provide practical advice to help clients, contractors and other dutyholders understand and manage the requirements relevant to their role.

RAMS & Method Statements

Clear Risk Assessments and Method Statements help explain the hazards associated with the work and the precautions and safe working methods to be followed.

Construction Phase Plans

Where required, a Construction Phase Plan sets out the Health & Safety arrangements, site rules and control measures for the construction phase. We can help prepare or review plans so they reflect the actual project.

Site Inspections

Our inspections focus on significant risks and practical site conditions, with clear findings and recommendations for improvement.
